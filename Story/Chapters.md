# Chapter Outline

The current structure contains five chapters. Individual objectives, puzzle solutions, encounter timings, and exact transitions remain open for level-design development.

## Chapter 1 — Arrival

**Purpose:** Introduce Daniel, the mystery, and the abandoned hospital.

- Establish the anonymous email, secret package, and disturbing phone call.
- Daniel reaches Hollow Ward.
- The player learns the basic exploration and survival controls.
- The hospital appears abandoned, but small clues suggest otherwise.

**Key mood:** Isolation and uncertainty.  
**Detailed objectives:** TBD.

## Chapter 2 — Forgotten Ward

**Purpose:** Begin connecting Daniel to the facility.

- Explore patient areas and discover records.
- Find evidence that Daniel may have been a patient.
- Introduce the wolf as a serious threat.
- Build tension through environmental clues and audio.

**Key mood:** Recognition and unease.  
**Detailed objectives:** TBD.

## Chapter 3 — Truth

**Purpose:** Reveal that Daniel's past is tied to Hollow Ward's experiments.

- Discover more research reports and personal records.
- Learn that Daniel was involved in the experiments.
- Establish that his memories are incomplete.
- Deepen the mystery around the anonymous scientist.

**Key mood:** Personal dread and confusion.  
**Detailed objectives:** TBD.

## Chapter 4 — Restricted Area

**Purpose:** Bring the player closer to the central research and its consequences.

- Enter restricted parts of the facility.
- Find information about the ECHO initiative and its related research.
- Discover more about the wolf's experimental nature.
- Preserve the wolf's connection to Daniel as a mystery.

**Key mood:** Escalation and revelation.  
**Detailed objectives:** TBD.

## Chapter 5 — Escape

**Purpose:** Deliver the final survival sequence and close the first game's immediate journey.

- Daniel attempts to escape Hollow Ward.
- Resolve the immediate threat of the facility and the scientist's attempt to help.
- Leave selected questions unanswered for a possible continuation.

**Key mood:** Urgency, survival, and unresolved mystery.  
**Exact ending and final cutscene:** TBD.

---

## Chapter design principles

- Each chapter should add new information, not repeat the same reveal.
- Documents should support exploration and never become the only way to understand essential events.
- Chase sequences should be spaced with quieter exploration sections.
- Keep the wolf frightening through anticipation, sound, and behaviour—not constant appearances.
- Protect the major Daniel/wolf connection and the full head-injury explanation from premature disclosure.
