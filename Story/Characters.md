# Characters

## Daniel Mercer

**Role:** Protagonist  
**Occupation/background:** Investigator  
**Approximate age:** 27  
**Appearance direction:** Male character wearing a long, dark coat; carries a flashlight.

### Known background

Daniel receives an email and a secret package from an anonymous scientist. After a disturbing nighttime phone call, he travels to Hollow Ward.

As the story progresses, Daniel discovers that he was once a patient at the hospital and was involved in its experiments. He has no clear memory of this period.

A head injury is connected to his missing memories. Its exact cause and full explanation are intentionally not disclosed in the first game.

### Character direction

Daniel should feel like an investigator trying to stay composed while confronting increasingly personal evidence. His reactions should develop from curiosity and suspicion toward fear, confusion, and determination.

## The Wolf

**Role:** Main roaming threat / experimental creature  
**Nature:** Large, powerful, wolf-like biological experiment

### Established facts

- The creature is the most physically powerful threat in the current concept.
- It is connected to Hollow Ward's biological experimentation.
- It can chase and overpower Daniel.
- When it catches Daniel, it knocks him unconscious rather than killing him.
- It says “Leave…” during an encounter.
- It has a hidden connection to Daniel.

### Protected mystery

The wolf was once Daniel's pet dog, but this connection is intended to remain secret in the first game and may be explored in a later story. The reason it spares Daniel is also a mystery.

**Still TBD:** Creature name, exact appearance details, behaviour rules, origin sequence, and how much of its past the player can infer in the first game.

## The Anonymous Scientist

**Role:** Inciting character; former researcher at Hollow Ward

### Established facts

- Sends Daniel an email and a secret package.
- Calls Daniel at night and asks for help; a scream is heard during the call.
- Was involved in Hollow Ward's experiments.
- Feels guilty about what happened.
- Formed a bond with Daniel.
- Wants to save Daniel, but the attempt comes at a price.

### Protected mystery

The scientist's identity and the full nature of the relationship with Daniel are not to be explained too early. The scientist's sacrifice or personal cost should have narrative consequences.

**Still TBD:** Name, appearance, exact position in the project, present location, and final fate.
