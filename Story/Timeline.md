# Story Timeline

This timeline separates established events from details that have not yet been fixed.

## Before the game

1. Hollow Ward operates as a psychiatric hospital and becomes associated with secret research. **Exact dates and institutional history: TBD.**
2. A research initiative known as ECHO is developed. Its full structure and timeline are still being refined.
3. Daniel is admitted to Hollow Ward as a patient.
4. Daniel becomes involved in the facility's experiments.
5. Daniel suffers a head injury connected to his missing memories. The exact cause is intentionally withheld from the first game.
6. Daniel's pet dog is subjected to biological experimentation and becomes connected to the wolf creature. The exact sequence is protected for a later reveal.
7. The scientist develops a bond with Daniel and later feels guilt over the experiments.
8. Events lead to Hollow Ward being abandoned or no longer operating normally. The exact shutdown sequence is **TBD**.

## Events immediately before gameplay

1. An anonymous scientist sends Daniel an email.
2. Daniel receives a secret package connected to Hollow Ward.
3. During the night, Daniel receives a phone call from the scientist.
4. A scream is heard, and the scientist asks Daniel for help.
5. Daniel travels to Hollow Ward.

## During gameplay

1. Daniel enters the abandoned facility.
2. He explores the hospital and finds records and other evidence.
3. He encounters the wolf.
4. He discovers evidence that he was once a patient at Hollow Ward.
5. He learns that he was involved in the experiments.
6. He reaches restricted areas and uncovers more about the research.
7. He attempts to escape.

**Exact order, timestamps, and chapter-to-event mapping:** To be finalized during narrative and level design.

## Future-story material

- The full explanation of Daniel's head injury.
- The complete history of Daniel's dog and the wolf.
- The reason the wolf spares Daniel.
- The full identity and history of the scientist.
- The complete cost and consequences of the scientist's attempt to save Daniel.

These are intentional mysteries, not missing facts to be filled in casually.
