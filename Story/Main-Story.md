# Hollow Ward — Main Story

## Overview

**Genre:** Survival horror  
**Format:** Single-player  
**Setting:** An abandoned psychiatric hospital known as Hollow Ward  
**Protagonist:** Daniel Mercer  
**Planned main-story length:** Approximately 1–1.5 hours  
**Primary development target:** PC

Hollow Ward is a story about memory, fear, identity, and the consequences of experiments that should never have happened.

## Premise

Daniel Mercer receives an unexpected email from an anonymous scientist. The message points him toward Hollow Ward and suggests that something important has been hidden there. A secret package follows, containing material connected to the abandoned facility.

Later, during the night, Daniel receives a phone call. The caller sounds frightened; a scream is heard, and the scientist asks Daniel for help. The call ends before Daniel can understand what is happening.

Daniel goes to Hollow Ward.

The hospital appears abandoned, but its corridors, records, and restricted areas suggest that its past is not finished with it. As Daniel explores, he finds patient records, research reports, audio logs, incident reports, and personal notes. These pieces gradually reveal that Hollow Ward was connected to a secret research initiative.

Daniel's own history is entangled with the facility. He was once a patient there and was involved in its experiments, but he cannot remember what happened. A past head injury is connected to his missing memories; the exact cause and full explanation are deliberately withheld from the first game's main reveal.

## The threat

A large, powerful wolf-like creature roams the facility. It is not an ordinary animal: it is connected to the hospital's biological experimentation.

The creature can pursue and overpower Daniel. When it catches him, it knocks him unconscious rather than killing him. On at least one encounter, it says:

> “Leave…”

Why it spares Daniel, and what it recognises in him, remain mysteries.

## The scientist

The anonymous scientist was involved in the experiments and feels guilty about what happened. The scientist had formed a bond with Daniel and now wants to save him, even if doing so comes at a personal cost.

The scientist's identity, exact history with Daniel, and the full price of the attempted rescue are not all revealed in the first game. These are protected story mysteries.

## Narrative direction

The story should unfold through exploration rather than long explanations. Environmental clues and in-game documents should let players connect details for themselves.

The intended emotional progression is:

1. **Uncertainty:** Daniel arrives without understanding the full danger.
2. **Recognition:** Familiar details and records suggest he has been here before.
3. **Dread:** The facility's research and the wolf's behaviour become harder to dismiss.
4. **Revelation:** Daniel discovers the connection between himself and Hollow Ward.
5. **Escape:** Daniel attempts to get out, while important questions remain unanswered.

## Themes

- Memory and identity
- Guilt and responsibility
- Fear and survival
- The difference between a monster and a victim
- The cost of trying to undo past mistakes

## Continuity rules

- Daniel was once a patient at Hollow Ward.
- Daniel was involved in the facility's experiments but does not remember them.
- The wolf is connected to biological experimentation and has a hidden connection to Daniel.
- The scientist wants to save Daniel and carries guilt over the past.
- The exact explanation of Daniel's head injury is not fully revealed in the first game.
- Unconfirmed details must remain marked **TBD** until deliberately approved.
