# Experiments — Story Reference

## Purpose

This file records the broad fictional themes of the experiments connected to Hollow Ward. It is intended for narrative consistency, not technical implementation.

## Memory and trauma

The research explores memory, trauma, and how fear can affect a person's behaviour. Daniel's missing memories make this theme central to the story.

**Confirmed connection:** Daniel was involved in the experiments and cannot remember his time as a patient.  
**Unresolved:** The exact events and mechanisms behind his memory loss.

## Fear and survival responses

The facility studied fear and survival behaviour as part of the wider fictional initiative. This theme can appear through researcher notes, patient observations, and the design of the hospital's restricted areas.

## Biological experimentation

The wolf is connected to biological experimentation at Hollow Ward. It is a powerful creature, and its existence demonstrates the dangerous consequences of the research.

**Protected reveal:** The wolf was once Daniel's pet dog. Keep this connection hidden in the first game's main narrative.

## Ethical consequences

The experiments should be portrayed as secretive and harmful, with responsibility shared across people and institutions. The scientist's guilt and desire to save Daniel are key emotional consequences.

## Writing guidelines

- Keep descriptions atmospheric and character-focused.
- Avoid detailed real-world procedures or technical recipes.
- Let documents reveal conflicting accounts and institutional cover-ups.
- Keep unapproved scientific explanations marked **TBD**.
