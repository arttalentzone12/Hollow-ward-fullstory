# ECHO — Fictional Research Initiative

## Overview

**ECHO** is the working name of the fictional research initiative connected to Hollow Ward.

Its broad narrative purpose is to explore memory, trauma, fear, behaviour, and survival responses. The project also has a fictional military-interest angle involving decision-making and survival under extreme conditions.

This is fictional worldbuilding for a horror game, not a description of real research procedures.

## Narrative purpose

ECHO provides the institutional reason behind the hidden research at Hollow Ward. It connects patient records, researcher notes, restricted areas, and Daniel's forgotten past.

The player should initially encounter ECHO as a name in scattered documents. Its larger purpose becomes clearer only as the player explores.

## Possible research branches

The following branch names have been discussed as a working structure:

- **ECHO:** Memory, trauma, and the effects of fear on behaviour.
- **ASCENSION:** Biological enhancement as a fictional research theme.
- **MIRROR:** Fear and survival-response themes.

These branch labels are provisional until the final project structure is locked.
