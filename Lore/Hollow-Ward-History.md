# Hollow Ward — Facility History

## Overview

Hollow Ward is an abandoned psychiatric hospital and the main setting of the game. Its ordinary hospital identity conceals a history of secret research.

The facility should feel like a real institution that was gradually overtaken by secrecy, restricted work, and the consequences of its experiments.

## Known story facts

- Daniel was once a patient at Hollow Ward.
- Daniel was involved in experiments conducted through the facility.
- The anonymous scientist worked within the research operation.
- The wolf is connected to biological experimentation.
- The facility contains patient records, research reports, audio logs, incident reports, and personal notes.
- Restricted areas hold important evidence about the research.

## Environmental storytelling

The hospital's history should be communicated through:
- Patient files and ward records.
- Research documents and authorization forms.
- Damaged or abandoned workspaces.
- Audio recordings and unanswered messages.
- Signs of hurried evacuation or institutional collapse.
- Contrasts between public hospital spaces and restricted research areas.

These details should create questions and gradually answer them. Avoid placing every explanation in a single exposition scene.

## Suggested facility areas

The current level-design concept includes:

1. **Reception:** The player's first impression of the hospital.
2. **Patient Ward:** Patient rooms, ward corridors, and records.
3. **Restricted Area:** Controlled-access spaces tied to the research.
4. **Research Laboratory:** Evidence of the experiments and ECHO.
5. **Escape Route:** The final path out of the facility.

These are proposed gameplay areas; exact floor plan, room count, and their chronological history are **TBD**.

## Unresolved history

- Founding date and original ownership.
- Public explanation for the hospital's closure.
- Exact relationship between the hospital administration and the research initiative.
- Who authorized the secret research.
- How the facility became abandoned.
- Whether any personnel remain during gameplay.

Do not treat possible explanations as canon until they are approved.
