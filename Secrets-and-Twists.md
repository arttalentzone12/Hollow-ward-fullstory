# Secrets & Protected Reveals

**Spoilers:** This file is intended for the developer and trusted collaborators. Do not use it as a player-facing summary.

## Protected reveals

### 1. Daniel's past

Daniel was once a patient at Hollow Ward and was involved in its experiments. He does not remember this period.

### 2. The head injury

Daniel's head injury is connected to his missing memories. The exact cause and full explanation are intentionally withheld from the first game.

### 3. The wolf's identity

The wolf was once Daniel's pet dog and was altered through the facility's biological experimentation. This connection should remain secret in the first game and may be explored in a later story.

### 4. Why the wolf spares Daniel

The wolf knocks Daniel unconscious rather than killing him and says “Leave…”. The reason for this behaviour is a mystery.

### 5. The scientist's relationship with Daniel

The anonymous scientist formed a bond with Daniel, feels guilty about the experiments, and wants to save him. The scientist's identity and full history are not to be revealed too early.

### 6. The scientist's price

The scientist's attempt to save Daniel comes at a cost. The exact sacrifice, consequence, and final outcome remain **TBD**.

## Reveal pacing

- Early game: establish the email, package, phone call, and abandoned hospital.
- Early-to-middle game: hint that Daniel has a personal connection to the facility.
- Middle game: confirm that Daniel was a patient and was involved in the experiments.
- Later game: reveal more about ECHO and the wolf's experimental nature.
- Ending: resolve the immediate escape story while preserving selected mysteries for a possible continuation.

## Unlocked vs. hidden information

The fact that Daniel was a patient and was involved in experiments is part of the first game's intended discovery.

The wolf's former identity as Daniel's dog, the full head-injury explanation, and the complete scientist backstory are protected reveals.

## Continuity warning

Do not casually explain a protected reveal in a collectible document, dialogue line, trailer, promotional image, or early cutscene. Any change to these reveals should be deliberate and reflected throughout the story bible.
